# Qasparr — Music Vault (Private)

A private mirror of one night's music work — 2026-10-26, Detroit time —
built with RESONANTIA, the author's own audio engine.

Authorship: Johnathan 'Qasparr' Monroe, ARR — All Rights Reserved, Without Prejudice.

This repository is **private** and is a vault, not a release. The album's
publication home is still to be named by the author.

## Map of the night

### `album/` — *Qasparr the Unfriendly Ghost*
- `qasparr-the-unfriendly-ghost-album.zip` — the first album, zipped:
  11 tracks, 37 files. Six tracks full (lyrics + pressed instrumental
  masters; seven masters counting both Hood Elegance cuts). Ten of eleven
  tracks lyric-complete after the ghost pass.
- `qasparr-the-unfriendly-ghost/` — the same album, unzipped: original
  `LYRICS.md` (byte-identical), `LYRICS-REIMAGINED.md`, `TRACKLIST.md`,
  `audio/` (WAV + 320k MP3 masters, Tracks 6–11), `lyrics-timed-lrc/`,
  `extras/` (sketches, covers, pilot).
- `qasparr-the-unfriendly-ghost-album-build-log.md` — the ultra-verbose
  build log.

Standing labels: every master is an **instrumental** — no final vocal mix
exists yet. Track 11's guide vocals are reference only. His verses are
byte-identical to his own; Lady Lilith's additions are marked
`[Lady Lilith]`; ghost-written verses are marked `[Ghost — for Qasparr]`
and are drawn only from his filed biography.

### `sigma-arpeggio/` — "Sigma Arpeggio" (Qasparr × Lady Lilith)
Full-length track built on the Fibonacci Arpeggio of the Solfeggio
Harmonies: `sigma-arpeggio-full-track-master.wav` (24-bit/96 kHz master;
measured −13.89 LUFS, true peak −1.50 dBTP, 4:36.5) plus the 320k phone
copy, the original first-sketch MP3, both vocal takes, the lyric sheet
(`FULL-TRACK-LYRICS.md`), the production log, the build script, and the
work reports (`work/*.json`).

Standing label: the vocals are **synthesized demo vocals** — his voice is
*imagined*, never recorded and never cloned; TTS delivery is spoken, not
beat-locked rap flow. The outro was forensically verified: UT 396 at a
1 Hz binaural offset, as scored.

### `brightmoor-rachets/` — "Brightmoor Rachets"
Detroit-bounce anthem (103.0 BPM, 3:32.04; master measured −14.38 LUFS,
true peak −1.18 dBTP): demo master WAV, 320k MP3, lyric sheet, production
log, the complete `pitch-kit/`, and `lakeyah-pitch-kit.zip`.

Standing label: the Lakeyah verse is a **GUIDE for an aspirational
feature slot** — a demo/pitch artifact only. No collaboration exists or
is claimed; her voice here is a third synthesized guide voice, and the
real verse is hers to write if her camp ever says yes.

### `juice-battle/` — "JUICE BATTLE (BAMF Scratch Duel)"
A Juice-parody DJ-battle scene (96.0 BPM, 2:22.0; −14.03 LUFS, true peak
−1.13 dBTP): demo master WAV, 320k MP3, production log, and the source
announcer clips under `work/BAMF/`.

Standing label: **PRIVATE DEMO — not for release.** It is built from the
author's own BAMF announcer pack (third-party announcer samples); any
commercial use would require clearance. The scratch side is a synthetic
homage in a classic turntablist style; no living DJ performed or is
affiliated.
