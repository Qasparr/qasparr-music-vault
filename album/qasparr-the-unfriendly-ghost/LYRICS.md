## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Reference match:** battle-rap announcer frame (intro/outro), chant hooks, dark minor key,
  hard drums, ~144 BPM halftime feel with double-time rap delivery, tight 2:30–2:40 runtimes.
  Three 16-bar verses ride the beat double-time like the reference — 16 bars ≈ 27s at 144.
- **"Vox Fantasma":** Latin verse staccato/percussive (imperial cadence), Greek verse smooth
  and rolling — the contrast IS the performance. Verse 3 merges them.
- **"Mama Made a Way":** pull the beat back half a notch, let it breathe — the album's heart.
- **"Gutter Benefit":** the closer and the thesis. The announcer's last line ("the winner is...
  ALL OF US") flips the reference track's battle frame into the unity message.
- **"Ride or Die":** Banshee's Eve-style verse is the energy peak — record it hungry.
- All hooks are 4-line chants — built to be shouted back, like the reference's hook.
